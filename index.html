Important Guidelines Before Filing a Plaint

1. Courts of Small Causes Jurisdiction Designed for quick recovery of debt, unpaid rent, bounced payments, or breach of contract. They do not handle land title disputes.
2. Verification Clause & Documents Every plaint must include a signed Verification clause and an attached list of evidence (invoices, WhatsApp chats, receipts).
