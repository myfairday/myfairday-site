Before you buy

A few things people ask

Do I need a lawyer to use these?

No — the templates are built so you can fill them in yourself. That said, they're general templates, not advice tailored to your case, so for anything high-value or contested, a quick consult with a local lawyer is worth it before you send.

What format do the templates come in?

Every template is delivered as an editable Word (.docx) document, styled and ready to fill in — with instructions and bracketed placeholders you replace with your own facts.

Will this guarantee I get paid?

No. A demand letter is a required first step in most small claims processes and often prompts payment on its own, but it doesn't guarantee an outcome — it puts your claim on record and, if needed, supports the court filing that follows.

Which country's version should I use?

Use the template for the country where you'll be filing the claim — usually where the person who owes you money is based, or where the agreement was made. If you're unsure, that's a good question to bring to a local lawyer.
